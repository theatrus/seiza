# Seiza 0.57.0

## Sharpening

`seiza model sharpen`, `SharpenModel` in Rust and Python, and
`seiza_sharpen_model_*` in the C ABI run a learned sharpening model. A
caller sets how much to narrow the stars and the rest of the image, with an
optional roll-off and an option to hold back broad change that lies under
the noise. The model reads a PSF field measured from the image itself. The
trainer and its record, up to the published `sharpen-v1-s102`, are in
`training/`. (#487, #522, #523)

## Stacking

- Lights can be chosen and weighted by their stars: `reference_score` now
  measures each light's PSF area and eccentricity, `select_frames` turns
  away outliers (cloud, few stars, elongated stars), and
  `LiveStacker::set_frame_weight_factors` weights the rest by sharpness.
  `seiza stack --select` does both. (#516)
- Every stack Seiza writes records `LIVETIME`, its accepted frames' summed
  exposure, and `FILTER`, when they share one. (#518)
- `reproject` puts an image on another WCS's grid, and a WCS maps whole
  arrays of points. (#515)
- Foraxx can smooth its blend factors, which removes OIII speckle. It is off
  by default. (#519)
- A 0–1 stack with a few star cores just above 1 stays on 0–1 for RC-Astro
  and PixInsight tools. It used to be taken for a camera frame, and
  BlurXTerminator returned zeros. The exchange map is now version 4. (#521)
- Many images go through RC-Astro in one start of its CLI. (#520)
- A continuum plan for starless images can set `target: starless`. Its
  pixel check is then reported but does not refuse the fit, since no star
  is left to keep a core or halo. (#524)
- `noise_at_scale` measures noise that neighbouring pixels share, which
  drizzle and resampling leave and `frame_noise` misses. `match_linear` fits
  a master's gain and offset to a reference, allowing for noise in both.
  (#507)

## Stretching

- The `ht` stretch clips and rescales as PixInsight's HistogramTransformation
  does, with a 24-bit lookup table for its midtones transfer. (#503)
- A stretch can be worked out on one image and applied to others, so they
  can be compared. The C ABI and Python now offer this too. (#510)

## Solving

A strongly distorted field keeps its SIP fit. The fit was refused when a
cubic inverse could not follow a cubic distortion to 0.1 px, and the solve
fell back to a linear solution with RMS of several arcseconds. The inverse
is now refined, and fitted at up to order 5 where needed. When a solve that
asked for SIP comes back linear, `sip_rejected` says why. (#511)

## Images and headers

- `read_image` reads a whole FITS or XISF image, every plane, in one call.
  (#512)
- `read_master_info` reads what a master records of its integration: total
  exposure, frames, sub-exposure, filter, integrator and drizzle scale, from
  PixInsight, Siril, Astro Pixel Processor and Seiza files, with where each
  value came from. (#517)

## Stars and backgrounds

- Elliptical PSFs are fitted from their moments, so stars stretched near 45°
  no longer come back round. (#506)
- Sensor tilt can be judged over many frames and told apart from a backfocus
  error. (#508)
- A background fit can correct one rectangle at a time. (#509)

## Compatibility

- A stacking context saved by this release records each frame's exposure
  and filter. An older Seiza refuses it. Older contexts open as before.
- Rust hosts that build `Solution`, `StackSnapshot`, `StackExportSnapshot`
  or `DrizzleResult` must add their new fields.
- Stacking, stretch, pipeline and model output versions are unchanged from
  0.56.0.

Dependency updates are included (#492, #493, #494).
