    Method and limitations

      Schema . Non-overlapping 16×16 patches at two-pixel pitch in the central 80%; only patches valid in every accepted frame contribute. Noise is the median patch MAD × 1.4826 of (odd sum − even sum)/N. At least 32 common patches are required. Odd depths have no split measurement. Robust noise describes the distribution core, not total outlier energy.
